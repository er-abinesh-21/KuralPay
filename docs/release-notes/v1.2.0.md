# KuralPay 1.2.0

More payments heard, fewer missed, and updates that install themselves.

## What's new

- **GPay Business payments are announced.** Shop QR payments like "₹20 received from Gunalan S" are now heard, with the right name
- **Missed payments are recovered.** If Android stops KuralPay to save memory, it comes back by itself and adds payments that arrived meanwhile. Turn on **Catch missed bank SMS** in Settings to also recover bank credit SMS
- **No double counting.** A bank credit sent twice by SMS is counted once
- **Forwarding:** add people with a name, see their number, and remove them safely. Forwarded payments now appear on Home with a ➤ mark and are not added to your own total
- **Automatic updates.** New versions install in the background while KuralPay isn't open and nothing is being announced. You can turn this off in Settings
- **Works on more phones.** Setup no longer gets stuck on phones that can't show the payment card over other apps (Android Go), and you can skip that step on any phone
- Fixes: changing your forwarding number, sending forwarding requests, labels on small screens

## Install

- Requires Android 12 or later.
- Already have KuralPay? Install over it. Your payments, settings and sign-in are kept. Do not uninstall first: uninstalling deletes the payment history stored on the phone.
- After this update, allow **Install unknown apps** for KuralPay once, so future updates can install automatically.
