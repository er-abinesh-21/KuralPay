# KuralPay 1.0.0

The first KuralPay release on Release Orbit. KuralPay speaks every payment you receive, so you never have to check your phone at the counter.

## What's new

- **Payment announcements:** every UPI and bank credit is spoken aloud in English or Tamil, with the payer's name
- **Bank SMS detection:** credits from bank SMS are announced; offers, reminders and debits are ignored
- **Payment card** shown over any app, plus replay of the last payments and of missed ones
- **Full-volume option:** announcements can raise the volume so they are always heard
- **Daily voice summaries** at times you choose
- **Account and backup:** sign in with email or Google; payments and settings are backed up to your account, with a deletion period you control
- **Payment forwarding:** payments can also be announced on another KuralPay phone
- **In-app updates:** new versions are checked, verified and installed from inside the app

## Install

- Requires Android 12 or later.
- Download `KuralPay-v1.0.0.apk` below and open it. Android asks once for permission to install apps from your browser or file manager.
- `KuralPay-v1.0.0.apk.sha256` is the SHA-256 checksum of the APK.
- Already have KuralPay? Install over it. Your payments, settings and sign-in are kept. Do not uninstall first: uninstalling deletes the payment history stored on the phone.
