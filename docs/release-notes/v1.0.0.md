# KuralPay 1.0.0

First release on Release Orbit.

- Hear every UPI and bank credit payment aloud, in English or Tamil
- Bank SMS detection with offers, reminders and debits ignored
- Payment card over any app, replay and missed-payment replay
- Daily voice summaries at times you choose
- Sign in with email or Google; payments and settings backed up to your account
- Payment forwarding to other KuralPay phones
- In-app updates from Release Orbit
