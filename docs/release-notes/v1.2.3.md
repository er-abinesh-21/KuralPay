# KuralPay 1.2.3

Correct payment times, and help when the phone keeps closing KuralPay.

## What's new

- **Correct times:** payments are recorded at the time they arrived. Before, a payment app showing an old notification again could move yesterday's payment into today's total
- **Keep KuralPay running:** if your phone's battery saver keeps stopping KuralPay, a card on Home shows the exact steps for your phone (Vivo, Xiaomi, Oppo, Samsung and others) and opens the right settings
- No double counting when a payment app shows the same notification again after KuralPay restarts

## Install

- Requires Android 12 or later.
- Already have KuralPay? Install over it. Your payments, settings and sign-in are kept. Do not uninstall first: uninstalling deletes the payment history stored on the phone.
